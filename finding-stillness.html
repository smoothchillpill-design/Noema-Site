Finding Stillness in a Loud World

Mindfulness works...and I can prove it right now.

Try to hone in on the furthest sound you can hear or focus on the sensation of your toes. If you truly immerse yourself in either, you will notice that it takes you out of your day-to-day thoughts unless you've already been practicing the art of mindfulness.
